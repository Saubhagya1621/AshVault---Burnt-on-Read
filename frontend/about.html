How AshVault Works

Zero Trust.
Zero Residue.

How your sensitive data is stored once, shared once, and permanently erased — by design.

The Process

From input to ash

Step 01 ✍️
You write your secret

Paste your API key, password, private note, or any sensitive text into AshVault's editor. Nothing leaves your browser until you confirm.

📝 Stored in memory until submission
Step 02 🔗
A unique UUID link is generated

A cryptographically random UUID v4 is generated and returned as your shareable link. This ID is impossible to guess — it's the only key to your vault.

🎲 UUID v4 · 2¹²² possible combinations
Step 03 ⏱
TTL timer starts ticking

MongoDB's TTL index automatically expires and deletes your secret after the chosen duration — 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days — even if it's never read.

⚙️ MongoDB TTL Index · Automated cleanup
Step 04 👁
Recipient opens the link

When the recipient visits the unique URL, the server looks up the secret and verifies it hasn't already been read and the TTL hasn't expired.

🔎 Single lookup · No residue
Step 05 🔥
Burn-on-Read — secret deleted instantly

The moment the secret is served, it is permanently and irreversibly deleted from the database. No copies, no logs, no recovery. It's ash.

💥 findOneAndDelete · Atomic · Irreversible
